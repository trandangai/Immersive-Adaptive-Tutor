Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Application: Immersive Adaptive Tutor (https://aitechvibething.me)

1. Overview

Welcome to Immersive Adaptive Tutor ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you access our service at https://aitechvibething.me.

2. Data We Collect

  • Google Authentication Profile: When you log in with Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, full name, profile picture URL, and a unique identifier from Google.
  • Educational & Session Data: Your learning roadmaps, comprehension scores, knowledge mastery levels, flashcards, and conversation logs are stored to provide continuity in your study experience.
  • Audio & Document Input: Study materials you paste or upload and your voice input during active voice sessions are processed in real time to generate adaptive Socratic explanations.

3. Google User Data & Google Drive Integration (Limited Use Policy)

Our application requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly scope to let you select and import study documents (e.g. PDF or Docs) from your Google Drive into your tutor sessions.

  • Strict Read-Only Access: We only access documents that you explicitly choose in the Drive selector. We never modify, overwrite, delete, or create files on your Google Drive.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Data: We do not sell, rent, or disclose Google user data or your files to any third parties or advertisers.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Tokens: OAuth credentials are kept in memory only for the duration of the active session.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: Immersive Adaptive Tutor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to provide, improve, and secure the educational features of the application, including generating customized roadmaps, running voice tutoring sessions, and tracking flashcard mastery.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your study records only while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at trandangai@gmail.com. All personal data will be completely erased from our database within 30 days of request.
